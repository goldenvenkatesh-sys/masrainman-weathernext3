EW index: how unusual the forecast 24-h rain is for that place and time of year — 0 is an ordinary day, 0.5 clearly unusual, 0.8 and above extreme, 1 wetter than any day in the 1991–2020 climate. It is the ECMWF Extreme Forecast Index formula against an ERA5 1991–2020 daily climate (±15 days, 0.25°). ECMWF ENS, AIFS ENS and CMC GEPS use their members (a true ensemble EFI); GEFS and WeatherNext 3 use the ensemble mean, which smooths rain, so their index runs a little lower; the other models are single runs. Days end at 08:30 IST (IMD rain day). Shown where the model gives at least 1 mm. The same index for every 24 h of the WeatherNext 3, IFS, AIFS, GFS and AIGFS runs is also a layer on the model maps page. These charts are not official forecasts — please follow IMD for forecasts and warnings.
