How the blend works

Two separate blends: Ensemble (ensemble means of ECMWF ENS, WeatherNext 3, AIFS ENS, GEFS, GEPS) and Deterministic (single runs of IFS HRES, AIFS, ICON, GFS). Every model is regridded to 0.1° and weighted; weights are shared out again over the models that reach each day. Days end at 08:30 IST.
Multi-model guidance from ECMWF Open Data (CC BY 4.0), NOAA GFS/GEFS, DWD ICON, ECCC GEPS and WeatherNext 3 — experimental, not official IMD warnings or forecasts. Analysis & visualisation: MasRainman. Maps follow India's boundaries as per the Government of India.
