Satellite radar passes

Caption
The GPM satellite's radar (NASA/JAXA) measures rain at every height, from the sea up to about 18 km, in a 245 km strip under the satellite. Once a day the passes of the last 24 hours over the Bay of Bengal, the Arabian Sea and South India are checked, and the ones with the most heavy rain are drawn in 3D. The satellite passes a place only once or twice a day, so some days have no picture. Kept: the last 30 passes.
