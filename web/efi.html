How to read it. The EFI compares the forecast distribution with the 1991–2020 ERA5 climate for the same time of year. 0 = normal, ±1 = the whole forecast is beyond the climate record. 0.5–0.8 means unusual, above 0.8 very unusual. The Shift of Tails (contours) shows how far beyond the climate 99th percentile the top 10 % of the forecast goes: 0 = it just reaches it, 1 or more = a rare event. Forecast spread comes from WeatherNext 3's 10/25/50/75/90th percentiles. Experimental, not an official warning: follow IMD.
